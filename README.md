Here I created a Pivot Table and Pivot chart by the dataset. In this project there are 4 tables and chart where I show sales by Category , total revenue and profit , yearly sales trends and department wise revenue.
 1:- Sales by category - for creating a table I insert a pivot table then choose category and total order ID of sales after that I got the whole category and sum of order ID where its show the total no. of  sales of category.

2:- Total revenue and profit - for this table in pivot table I choose the total sales and total profit.

3:- Yearly sales trends - for creating this firstly I used to sort the dataset after that add a new column of year then from the order date I choose the year function to get the year.
After that I inserted a pivot table choose the category , sub-category and year for getting the yearly sales.

4:-Department wise Revenue - I inserted a pivot table and choose the category and sub-category to generate the sales revenue.

After creating the whole pivot tables I used to generate charts for visual analysis and its really help to analyze the data very easily.
